# Fishing Adventure Depths

A fishing game that runs in a web browser as a single HTML file. It works on desktop and on iPad.

Walk the deck of your boat, cast, and steer your lure down winding cave pathways. Ram fish with the lure to weaken them, reel them in along your line, and grab them by the lip at the side of the boat.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser (Safari, Chrome or Edge).

## Features

- 39 real fish species in six places, from a home pond to the deep sea (25 m to 500 m deep)
- Cave pathways lead to deeper water, and the deeper you go, the bigger the fish
- The fishing line trails along the path your lure took. Each rod holds a set length of line.
- Weather (rain, storms, fog, snow) and a day/night cycle that affect which fish bite
- Upgrades for your rod, lure, fishing line, lure light, fish finder and cooler
- Sound is generated in the browser, so there are no extra files to download

Progress is saved automatically in the browser.
